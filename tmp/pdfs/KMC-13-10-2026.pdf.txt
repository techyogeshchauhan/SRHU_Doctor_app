Kangaroo Mother Care (KMC) is early, continuous and prolonged skin-to-skin contact between a preterm or low birth weight newborn and the 
mother (or an alternate caregiver), combined with exclusive breastmilk feeding, early discharge when appropriate, and structured follow-up.
Except if the baby is unable to breathe spontaneously after resuscitation, is in shock or needs mechanical ventilation

HOME KMC COUNSELLING
• Continue KMC as long as possible 
• Continue exclusive breastfeeding 
• Encourage family participation 
• Attend scheduled follow-up 
• Seek care if “danger signs” develop
• Go to Neonatal Triage STW Module

• Start KMC early in every eligible preterm or low-birth-weight 
baby; continue as long as possible
• Ensure correct positioning, a clear airway, and secure support; 
monitor temperature, breathing, and feeding
• Support exclusive breast milk feeding and counsel families to 
continue KMC at home with scheduled follow-up
• Do not initiate KMC in babies unable to breathe spontaneously 
after resuscitation, in shock, or requiring mechanical ventilation
• Do not routinely stop KMC for oxygen, CPAP, IV therapy, or tube 
feeding
• Do not delay KMC because the mother is unavailable; involve a 
trained alternate caregiver
