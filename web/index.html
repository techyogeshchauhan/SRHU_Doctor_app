STWNeo
Neonatal clinical decision support
Install STWNeo PWA

Add to your iPhone Home Screen for offline bedside access

  1. 1 Open the application in Safari.
  2. 2 Tap the Share button in the bottom bar.
  3. 3 Select “Add to Home Screen” ⊞.
  4. 4 Tap “Add” in the top-right corner to install the PWA.
  5. 5 The STWNeo app icon will appear on your iPhone Home Screen.
